## Riff blocks
The five building blocks (Quick Dip, Swift Lift, Swoopy Swift Lift, Quick Dip Trip, Skip Quick Dip Trip) come from Crystal Cherelle of Indie Artist School: [Master 5 R&B Riffs Every Pro Singer Uses | Building Block Method](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=saWqsqZUy5M) and her [Riff Builder](https://riffbuilder.indieartistschool.com).

## Progressions, licks, and chord-scale engine
Shared with [Vocallicks](https://github.com/rjbrown85/Vocallicks). Each progression's source (David Bennett Music Theory, Hooktheory, Open Music Theory, and others) is listed in `js/data.js` and in the Vocallicks credits.

## Avoid notes
The yellow "avoid note" grade follows the jazz convention of a scale note a half step above a chord tone. Background: [Avoid note (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avoid_note), [The Jazz Piano Site: Avoid Notes](https://www.thejazzpianosite.com/jazz-piano-lessons/jazz-improvisation/avoid-notes/).
